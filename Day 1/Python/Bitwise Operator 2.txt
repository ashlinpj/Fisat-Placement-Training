RIGHT AND LEFT SHIFT TRICK

Bit Shift Trick

Left Shift:
n << k = n × 2^k

Example:
10 << 1

= 10 × 2
= 20

Binary:
1010 → 10100

Adding one 0 at the end doubles the number.

---

Right Shift:
n >> k = n / 2^k

Example:
20 >> 1

= 20 / 2
= 10

Binary:
10100 → 1010
Removing one digit from the end halves the number.
